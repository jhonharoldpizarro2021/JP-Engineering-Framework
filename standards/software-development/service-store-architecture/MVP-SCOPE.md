# MVP Scope

## Included

1. Colombia storefront in Spanish.
2. Service catalog.
3. Service detail pages.
4. Fixed-price, starting-price, and quotation-required service modes.
5. Service-specific intake form.
6. Cart and checkout.
7. COP currency.
8. Customer registration and login.
9. Legal and privacy consent.
10. One Colombian payment gateway selected through an ADR.
11. Order confirmation.
12. Customer order history.
13. Bagisto administration for catalog and orders.
14. Service-order status workflow.
15. Email notifications.
16. WhatsApp and contact support links.
17. Mobile, tablet, and desktop QA.
18. Sentry error monitoring.
19. Consent-controlled PostHog and GTM integration.
20. Service availability or waiting-list flow.

## Deferred

- Native mobile application.
- Laravel Passport.
- FCM push notifications.
- International storefront activation.
- Multiple local payment gateways in the first release.
- Automated appointment scheduling.
- Marketplace with multiple service providers.
- Subscription billing.
- Advanced CRM.
- Full CRO automation.
- Complex loyalty program.

## Initial service-order statuses

- pending-payment
- paid
- requirements-pending
- requirements-complete
- under-review
- in-progress
- client-validation
- completed
- cancelled
- refunded

The final status machine requires approval before implementation.
